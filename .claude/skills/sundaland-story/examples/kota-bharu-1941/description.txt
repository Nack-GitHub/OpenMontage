Seventy-eight minutes before the first bombs fell on Pearl Harbor (by most accounts), Japanese troops were already storming a beach in Malaya. This is the story of Kota Bharu, 8 December 1941, and why the Pacific War was really a war for Sundaland's oil, rubber and tin.
