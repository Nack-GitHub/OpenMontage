# Format `wild`: Wild Trade (pilot)

## Identity
- **Series:** Wild Trade · playlist "Wild Trade"
- **Title pattern:** "A Nature Documentary, But the Animals Are <Things>" · alt "Planet Ocean: The Great <Thing> Migration"
- **Hook device:** a hushed wildlife reveal ("out of the haze, something enormous appears") + the first Species card.
- **Voice persona:** Brian, **hushed nature-documentary** delivery: ElevenLabs speed **0.90–0.92**, higher
  stability, 0.5 s between sentences. **Never imitate or name a real presenter's voice.** It's only the genre's tone.
- **Length:** 7–9 min. Speech is slower (~145–150 wpm), so the budget is ≈1,050–1,250 words.

## Spine
```
Cold open: dawn reveal + Species card (≈45 s) → globe → sting
→ 5–7 CHAPTERS of the "life cycle": feeding ground → long swim → the dangerous pass (chokepoint) → gathering place
  (hub) → journey's end → epilogue (the graveyard / rebirth)
→ "the migration never ends" → shell outro → end card
```
- The metaphor maps **one-to-one** to real logistics: feeding = loading, migration = trade route, predators/obstacles
  = draft limits, weather, chokepoints; herd = fleet; molting = drydock; graveyard = ship-breaking.
- The comedy comes from playing it **completely straight**. No winking lines and no jokes in the narration. The
  humour lives in the Species cards' fake Latin names and the deadpan tone.
- Budget: ~150–200 words per chapter.

## Shot grammar additions
- **Chapter card** replaces the numeral card: chapter title in Fraunces *italic* over slow footage with a warm
  wildlife grade, kicker `WILD TRADE · CHAPTER 3 · THE NARROW PASS`.
- Footage is slower than in facts (shots of 4–6 s, slow-mo where available), with fewer cuts.
- Map scenes show the fleet as **many moving dots (a herd)** around the followCam vessel.

## Signature component: `SpeciesCard`
A wildlife-documentary info card: common name (Fraunces) · *fake binomial* (Fraunces italic, e.g. *Petroleum
gigantus*) · 3–4 stat rows in Plex Mono (LENGTH, CARRIES, RANGE, LIFESPAN) · thin teal rule and the amber key
number. It slides in from the left and holds ≥4 s. A new card appears whenever a new "species" shows up (VLCC → ULCC).

## Topic rules + episode ideas
Real fleets or flows **through Sundaland/Asia** with a life cycle.
1. Oil tankers: Gulf → Malacca/Lombok → East Asia (draft in `examples/wild-tanker-v1.md`)
2. Container-ship "herds" and the Singapore anchorage
3. The monsoon traders: the historical dhow/junk migration on seasonal winds (Malacca's origin)
4. Bulk carriers and the coal/iron-ore flow (Australia → Asia, via Lombok/Makassar)
5. Fishing fleets and the Andaman tuna run (economics of the catch, nothing about illegal fishing)

## Sound
Orchestral / ambient nature music from the Audio Library. **Natural ambience** (wind, waves, gulls) instead of
whooshes in the cold open. Keep whoosh/pop SFX quieter than in facts (−6 dB) so they don't break the tone.

## Thumbnail
A low-angle golden-light shot of the "animal" + a Species-card frame with the fake Latin name + ≤3 words
("THE GREAT MIGRATION").

## Guardrails
Mention origins only geographically ("the Gulf"). No Hormuz tension, no dark fleet or sanctions, no piracy.
